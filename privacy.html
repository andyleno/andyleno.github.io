George privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

George is a personal voice assistant used by one person, its owner, on their own computer. This policy explains what George does with Google user data.

What George accesses

Only the owner's Google Calendar events, through the Google Calendar API, using the calendar.events permission the owner grants when signing in. George reads events so it can answer questions such as "what's on tomorrow?", and creates or updates events when the owner asks. Every change is read back to the owner and only made after they say yes.

How it's used

Calendar details are used only to answer the owner's own requests. To understand a request and reply by voice, the relevant event details are sent to the AI services George is built on: Anthropic (Claude), which works out the answer, and ElevenLabs, which speaks it. They are not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone else, and no person reads them.

Storage

The Google sign-in token is stored on the owner's computer in a file only their user account can read. George does not keep a copy of the calendar; it reads events from Google when asked.

Google API Services User Data Policy

George's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect George at any time. george calendar logout google deletes the stored token from their computer, and removing George at Google Account → Third-party connections revokes its access at Google.
